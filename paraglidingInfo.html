Paragliding

Soar like a bird and witness breathtaking views as you glide effortlessly through the sky. Paragliding is the perfect blend of freedom and thrill, offering a surreal connection with the wind and the world below.

🛡️ Safety Measures

  • All equipment certified and maintained to international standards.
  • Trained and licensed tandem pilots guide the flight.
  • Detailed pre-flight briefing and weather condition checks.
  • Helmets, harnesses, and radio communication used for safety.
  • Emergency landing protocols in place.

🎯 Prerequisites

  • No prior experience required for tandem flights.
  • Comfortable with heights and basic fitness preferred.
  • Not recommended for those with severe vertigo or heart conditions.
  • Consent form and safety declaration mandatory.

📋 Age & Weight Limit

Age Requirement: 12 years and above

Weight Limit: 30 kg to 95 kg

⏱️ Flight Duration

Typical flight time ranges from 10–30 minutes depending on wind conditions and flight type. The entire experience including prep takes about 1 hour.

✨ Why You’ll Love It

  • Feel the freedom of flying without an engine.
  • Enjoy panoramic mountain, valley, or ocean views.
  • Great for first-time flyers and adventurers alike.
