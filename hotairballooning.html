Hot Air Ballooning

Rise above the world and float peacefully through the skies with our magical Hot Air Ballooning experience. Perfect for dreamers, couples, and photographers — this journey offers spellbinding views and a tranquil escape.

🛡️ Safety Measures

  • Flights are conducted by DGCA-certified balloon pilots.
  • Real-time wind and weather monitoring for safe takeoff and landing.
  • Balloon and basket are maintained under strict safety protocols.
  • Emergency landing kits and communication systems on board.
  • Pre-flight briefing for all passengers before boarding.

🎯 Prerequisites

  • No prior flying experience needed.
  • Passengers should be able to stand for at least 1 hour.
  • Not recommended for pregnant women or individuals with severe back/knee problems.
  • Flat, closed-toe shoes and layered clothing recommended.

📋 Age & Weight Limit

Age Requirement: 5 years and above

Weight Limit: Up to 110 kg per person (may vary slightly by location)

📍 Flight Duration

The flight typically lasts between 45 minutes to 1 hour depending on wind speed and terrain. The entire activity including setup and landing may take around 2 to 3 hours.

✨ Why You’ll Love It

  • Silent, serene floating with panoramic sunrise or sunset views.
  • Great for photography, proposals, and peaceful escapes.
  • Popular in Jaipur, Lonavala, and Hampi for scenic views from the skies!
