White Water Rafting

Navigate the roaring rapids of India's wild rivers with an exhilarating white water rafting adventure! Perfect for adrenaline junkies and nature lovers, this is your chance to paddle through pure thrill.

🛡️ Safety Measures

  • Certified guides accompany every raft.
  • Life jackets and helmets provided for every participant.
  • Briefing and paddle training before launch.
  • Rescue kayaks and safety crews on standby.
  • Trips conducted only in safe water levels and weather conditions.

🎯 Prerequisites

  • Basic swimming skills preferred but not mandatory.
  • Fitness level: Moderate to good stamina recommended.
  • No severe heart, back, or respiratory issues.
  • Comfortable water-friendly clothing and footwear.

📋 Age & Weight Limit

Age Requirement: 14 years and above

Weight Limit: 35 kg to 100 kg

📍 Rafting Duration

Rafting stretches range from 5 km to 20 km, taking about 1 to 3 hours based on the grade of rapids. The full experience including safety drill and return transport may take 3-4 hours.

✨ Why You’ll Love It

  • Thrilling rapids from Grade II to Grade IV!
  • Splash through scenic gorges, forests, and riverbanks.
  • An unforgettable team adventure with raw nature power!
