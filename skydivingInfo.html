Skydiving

Plunge from the skies at 13,000 feet and feel the ultimate rush as you freefall through the clouds. Skydiving is the ultimate adventure to conquer fear and experience flight like never before.

🛡️ Safety Measures

  • Highly experienced instructors with thousands of jumps.
  • Certified parachutes and automatic activation devices (AAD).
  • Pre-jump training, gear checks, and flight simulations.
  • Tandem jumps with securely attached instructor for beginners.
  • Medical staff and safety crew always on standby.

🎯 Prerequisites

  • No previous skydiving experience required for tandem jumps.
  • Must be physically fit and free from heart or respiratory conditions.
  • Mandatory safety briefing and consent form signing.
  • Alcohol or drug use before jump strictly prohibited.

📋 Age & Weight Limit

Age Requirement: 18 years and above

Weight Limit: Up to 100 kg (varies slightly by location)

⏱️ Jump Duration

Freefall lasts around 40–60 seconds, followed by a 5–7 minute parachute glide. The full session including briefing and gear-up takes around 2 hours.

✨ Why You’ll Love It

  • Adrenaline unlike anything you’ve ever felt.
  • Spectacular bird’s-eye views from thousands of feet above.
  • A powerful confidence-boosting experience.
