Wildlife Safari Locations in India

Ranthambore, Rajasthan

One of the best places to spot tigers in their natural habitat, with ancient ruins and lakes.

Kanha National Park, Madhya Pradesh

Dense forests and grassy meadows home to tigers, barasingha, and leopards.

Kaziranga, Assam

Famous for the one-horned rhinoceros and rich biodiversity in the floodplains of the Brahmaputra.

Jim Corbett, Uttarakhand

India’s oldest national park, nestled in the foothills of the Himalayas, ideal for tiger sightings.
