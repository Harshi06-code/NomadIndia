Scuba Diving

Dive into a world of vibrant coral reefs, exotic marine life, and surreal silence beneath the surface. Scuba diving takes you into the heart of the ocean for an unforgettable underwater journey.

🛡️ Safety Measures

  • PADI/SSI certified dive masters and instructors.
  • Briefing on underwater communication and emergency procedures.
  • Top-grade gear including tanks, wetsuits, fins, and regulators.
  • Controlled depth and duration for beginners and first-timers.
  • Medical kits and oxygen available on dive boats.

🎯 Prerequisites

  • No swimming experience needed for Discover Scuba Diving (DSD).
  • Basic physical fitness and comfort in water preferred.
  • Not recommended for individuals with respiratory or heart conditions.
  • Medical declaration and consent form mandatory.

📋 Age & Depth Limit

Age Requirement: 10 years and above

Dive Depth: Up to 12 meters for beginners

⏱️ Dive Duration

Underwater exploration lasts about 30–45 minutes. The full experience including training and boat ride takes approximately 2–3 hours.

✨ Why You’ll Love It

  • Explore magical underwater ecosystems and marine creatures.
  • Weightless and meditative underwater feeling.
  • Perfect adventure for ocean lovers and first-time divers.
