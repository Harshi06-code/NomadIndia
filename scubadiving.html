Scuba Diving Locations in India

Havelock Island, Andaman

Crystal clear waters, rich coral reefs, and diverse marine life — a diver's paradise.

Grande Island, Goa

Explore shipwrecks and underwater life along the scenic Goan coast.

Pondicherry

Colorful corals and calm waters make this French-influenced town a great dive spot.

Kovalam, Kerala

Dive into the Arabian Sea and witness reef life on the southern coast of India.
