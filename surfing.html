Surfing Locations in India

Kovalam Beach, Kerala

One of India’s oldest surfing destinations, great for both beginners and experienced surfers.

Mulki, Karnataka

A calm surf town with gentle waves and excellent surf schools along the coast.

Varkala, Kerala

Cliffside waves and relaxing vibes make this a perfect spot for soulful surfing.

Auroville, Tamil Nadu

Peaceful beaches and consistent waves draw surfers from around the world.
