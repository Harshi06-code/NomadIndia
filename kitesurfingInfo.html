Kitesurfing

Harness the power of the wind and ride the waves like never before! Kitesurfing is an electrifying fusion of surfing, wakeboarding, and paragliding—perfect for thrill-seekers who love the sea and sky.

🛡️ Safety Measures

  • Certified kitesurfing instructors for all experience levels.
  • Use of safety-release systems and floatation vests.
  • Training on wind direction, body dragging, and emergency landings.
  • Lessons conducted in shallow water zones for beginners.
  • First aid and rescue support on standby.

🎯 Prerequisites

  • Basic swimming ability is required.
  • No prior experience needed—intro lessons available.
  • Participants should be in good physical health.
  • Mandatory waiver and safety form submission.

📋 Age & Wind Conditions

Age Requirement: 14 years and above

Wind Conditions: Ideal with steady 12–25 knots

⏱️ Session Duration

A typical kitesurfing session ranges from 2 to 3 hours including setup, wind briefing, water time, and instructor guidance.

✨ Why You’ll Love It

  • Experience the thrill of flight over waves.
  • Perfect mix of wind, water, and wild energy.
  • Great for adrenaline junkies and wind lovers alike.
