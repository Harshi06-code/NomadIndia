Surfing

Ride the rhythm of the ocean as you glide over rolling waves! Surfing blends skill, thrill, and balance for a liberating water sport experience powered by nature.

🛡️ Safety Measures

  • Certified surf instructors for all skill levels.
  • Use of soft-top boards for beginners to ensure safety.
  • Training on ocean currents, wave etiquette, and basic rescue techniques.
  • Surfing areas chosen based on tide and weather for optimal safety.
  • First aid and lifeguard support at all sessions.

🎯 Prerequisites

  • Basic swimming skills recommended.
  • No prior surfing experience needed for beginners.
  • Good physical fitness helps with paddling and balance.
  • Waiver and consent form required for participation.

📋 Age & Surf Zone Info

Age Requirement: 10 years and above

Surf Zones: Beginner-friendly beaches with waist-high waves

⏱️ Session Duration

Each session lasts about 1.5 to 2 hours, including warm-up, technique briefing, and water practice with instructor support.

✨ Why You’ll Love It

  • Feel the freedom of gliding with the waves.
  • Build strength, balance, and ocean confidence.
  • Perfect mix of sport and soul-soothing sea vibes.
