Bungee Jumping

Take the leap of a lifetime! Experience the heart-racing thrill of freefall as you jump off towering cliffs or bridges, secured only by a high-tension elastic cord. Bungee Jumping is not just a sport — it's pure adrenaline and fearless freedom.

🛡️ Safety Measures

  • Certified equipment tested to international safety standards.
  • Highly trained jumpmasters and support staff.
  • Full body harness and ankle cords used for maximum security.
  • Double safety checks before every jump.
  • Emergency medical services available onsite.

🎯 Prerequisites

  • Good physical health is required.
  • Not suitable for individuals with high blood pressure, heart conditions, or recent surgeries.
  • No alcohol consumption before the jump.
  • Signed waiver and consent form mandatory.

📋 Age & Weight Limit

Age Requirement: 14 years and above

Weight Limit: 40 kg to 110 kg

⏱️ Jump Duration

The actual freefall lasts around 5–10 seconds, but the entire experience from harnessing to rebound and recovery takes approximately 30 minutes.

✨ Why You’ll Love It

  • Conquer your fears in the most exhilarating way.
  • Feel the rush of weightlessness and freedom.
  • Capture once-in-a-lifetime memories and stories.
