River Rafting Locations in India

Rishikesh, Uttarakhand

The Ganges offers thrilling rapids and beautiful gorges — India's rafting capital.

Zanskar River, Ladakh

One of the most adventurous rafting spots with icy waters and dramatic landscapes.

Teesta River, Sikkim

Flowing through lush hills and gorges, perfect for intermediate-level rafting.

Barapole River, Coorg

A Southern gem for white-water rafting lovers, with thrilling grade IV rapids.
