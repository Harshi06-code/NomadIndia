Snorkeling Locations in India

Havelock Island, Andaman

Crystal-clear waters and coral reefs make this a premier snorkeling destination in India.

North Bay Island, Andaman

Famous for its shallow reefs, perfect visibility, and diverse marine life.

Elephant Beach, Andaman

Vibrant coral gardens just off the beach, perfect for casual snorkelers.

Netrani Island, Karnataka

Known as the 'Pigeon Island,' it offers a thrilling snorkeling experience off the west coast.
