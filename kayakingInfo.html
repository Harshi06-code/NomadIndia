Kayaking

Paddle your way through serene lakes, rushing rivers, or coastal waters! Kayaking is the perfect blend of peaceful nature exploration and adventurous water sport.

🛡️ Safety Measures

  • Certified guides and rescue-trained instructors accompany each trip.
  • Life jackets and helmets provided for all participants.
  • Route selection based on weather, tides, and participant experience.
  • Basic paddling and safety briefing before the activity.
  • Dry bags available for essentials; rescue boats nearby in open waters.

🎯 Prerequisites

  • Comfort with water is helpful but not mandatory.
  • No prior kayaking experience needed for flatwater routes.
  • Moderate fitness level preferred for river kayaking.
  • Waiver and health declaration form required.

📋 Age & Route Info

Age Requirement: 8 years and above (may vary by region)

Route Type: Calm lake, scenic river, or coastal kayaking options

⏱️ Session Duration

Depending on the route, kayaking trips range from 1 hour to 3 hours, including gear setup, briefing, and paddling.

✨ Why You’ll Love It

  • Peaceful glides and splashy thrills in one trip.
  • Explore hidden coves, mangroves, and backwaters.
  • Great for solo adventures or group bonding.
