Paragliding Locations in India

Bir Billing, Himachal Pradesh

World-famous paragliding site nestled in the Dhauladhar mountains with stunning aerial views.

Kamshet, Maharashtra

Just a short drive from Pune and Mumbai, ideal for beginners and weekend getaways.

Solang Valley, Manali

Soar over snowy peaks and pine forests in this Himalayan adventure paradise.

Mussoorie, Uttarakhand

Fly above the misty hills and valleys for a serene and scenic paragliding experience.
